# Competitor research: strength-training logbook apps (November 2026)

Scope: seven apps — Hevy, Liftoff, Strong, Fitbod, Alpha Progression, Boostcamp, JEFIT — researched for a Czech offline Flutter strength-training logbook. Sources are official sites, help centers, and App Store listings wherever possible; SEO review sites and search snippets are used only where primary pages were unavailable. Claims from a single weak source or details that could not be confirmed are marked `unverified`. Nothing here is a substitute for testing the current app build.

## 1. Hevy

**Positioning.** Free, ad-free workout tracker and planner with a social feed; App Store copy reports 15M+ lifters and 112M+ workouts logged in 2025. Review themes: very clean, fast logging with the previous weight/reps shown inline; large exercise library with demos; social accountability is the main reason users stay.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** `Workout` tab → `New Routine` or `+ Start Empty Workout`. Live session shows a stopwatch, completed-set count and volume load. Each set row has set number, `PREVIOUS`, `KG`/`LBS`, `REPS`/`REP RANGE` or `TIME`, optional `RPE`, and a completion checkbox. Tap the set number to open a type menu: Warm Up, Normal, Failure, Drop. Swipe left deletes a set; `+ Add Set` appends one.

**Set types & tools.** Warm-up sets can also be batch-added from the exercise 3-dot menu, together with reorder, replace, superset (more than two exercises) and remove. Pinned notes, routine notes and live-session notes accept clickable links. Rest timer is per exercise (5 s–5 min), with a global default under `Profile > gear > Workouts`, and can be turned off. Smart Superset Scrolling auto-scrolls to the next superset exercise. Plate calculator: enabled in settings, opened from the KG/LBS keyboard via `Calculator`; uses bar weight and available plates for barbell, short bar and EZ bar. Warm-up calculator is Pro-only; settings define plate/dumbbell rounding and the warm-up formula. Inline stopwatch sits under `TIME`. RPE is optional.

**Progression / coaching.** Originally a pure tracker; now Hevy Trainer (Pro) builds a program from goal, equipment, experience and preferences, and tracks consistency, time, volume, sets, muscle distribution and bodyweight. Release notes mention 12–20 rep targets for exercises that are hard to progress, plus cardio, injury and variety settings. Manual routines remain the core for free users.

**Progress & stats.** Exercise graphs over 3 months / year / all time; heaviest weight, true and projected 1RM, best set volume, best session volume, most reps, and set records broken down by rep count. Strength Level for squat/bench/deadlift. Live PR notifications. Muscle-distribution body diagram in sets per muscle. Monthly/yearly/multi-year calendars, streak tracking, progress photos, body measurements and share cards.

**Gamification.** Streaks, PRs, strength level, challenges/social feed, follower and workout sharing. No ranks or XP system confirmed.

**Home / navigation / design.** `Workout` and `Profile` tabs plus a social/feed area. Clean, dense-but-readable rows, blue primary CTA, large tap targets. Exact light/dark palette and typography `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free: no ads, unlimited logging, 26 programs in eight categories, 4 routines, 7 custom exercises, basic stats, social, rest timer, offline use, cloud backup. Pro: Trainer, unlimited routines and custom exercises, full history, advanced analytics; warm-up calculator is explicitly Pro. Some exact caps come from secondary reviews and are `unverified`.

## 2. Liftoff

**Positioning.** Ranked gym workouts — lifting with ranks, leaderboards, XP, daily quests and streaks. App Store review themes: “shockingly addictive”, “gamify the gym grind”; motivation comes from competition and never breaking a streak.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** Bottom `+` → choose exercises → enter sets and reps. Set number to the left of `PREV` opens warm-up/drop set. Swipe left deletes a set. Rest timer sits at the top-left of the tracker sheet; configure all tracked exercises from `Tracker > Settings > Default Exercise Rest Timer`. Units are set in Settings/Tracker or the `+` top-right. Bodyweight is required before posting a workout for rank calculation. Each exercise has a `How To Log` guide; library of ~600 exercises with video guides, including sets/reps/weight/RPE and supersets.

**Set types & tools.** Warm-up and drop sets, supersets, RPE, per-exercise rest, video guides. No plate or warm-up calculator confirmed in primary sources (`unverified`). Notes beyond per-exercise guidance `unverified`.

**Progression / ranking.** Rank formula uses estimated 1RM + bodyweight + sex → strength rating 1–1000 → nine ranks: Wood, Bronze, Silver, Gold, Platinum, Diamond, Champion, Titan, Olympian. Each rank has 0–100 LP; rank-up every 100 LP except Olympian. Liftoff Rank is the accumulated score across exercise ranks; ~400 of ~600 exercises are ranked. A workout generator builds single sessions or multi-week programs from goal, schedule, equipment and experience. This is not offline-first: cloud sync and AI food scan need internet.

**Progress & stats.** Exercise ranks, estimated 1RM, leaderboards by friends/country/world, deeper analytics in Pro. Exact chart types, heatmaps and body-measurement support `unverified` from primary sources.

**Gamification.** This is the category leader: streaks break after 3 days without posting; rest days count. Shop sells Super Restore, Mega Restore and Streak Revive eggs with a one-week restore window. XP Shakes reward the first post of the day. Seasons run every four months (Jan/May/Sep). Eggs are the currency; Jymbo is the mascot. Share cards, Strava posting and leaderboards complete the loop.

**Home / navigation / design.** Tracker-first bottom navigation with `+` as the primary action; rank and streak surfaces are prominent. Playful, colorful, game-like visual language. Exact layout details `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free: core logging, ranks, streaks, leaderboards and friends. Pro: AI food scanning and deeper analytics. Secondary source gives $3.99–$14.99/mo or $39.99–$79.99/yr, streak restores paid separately in eggs, no lifetime plan — pricing `unverified`.

## 3. Strong

**Positioning.** “Think less. Lift more.” The simplest/fastest barbell logger. Review themes: “bare bones and serious”, fastest set logging, no coaching or social distractions.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** Start empty or from a template. Exercises stay in order; Add Exercises, drag to reorder, `Add Set`, swipe to delete. Enter weight and reps, tap the checkbox to complete. `Notes` per workout; `Finish` top-right. Tap the Set Number in the `Set` column to tag a set: Warm-up (excluded from charts and metrics), Drop Set, Failure (an extra attempted failed rep; 0 reps means a failed 1RM attempt). RPE scale 6–10.

**Set types & tools.** Supersets shown by a vertical line at the left of grouped exercises: iPhone select multiple + `Superset`; Android `... > Create Superset` then the chain icon. `Next` jumps to the next set of the appropriate exercise. Rest timer defaults to 2:00 after a completed set, visible top-left; tap for full screen, skip or modify. Per-exercise More Menu sets separate warm-up and working rest durations; settings control sound. Warm-up Calculator (Pro) batch-adds warm-ups for barbell/dumbbell/machine with customizable formulas. Plate Calculator (Pro): tap weight field, then keyboard button; plates per side, default Olympic bar 20 kg/45 lb, bar type via More Menu. Notes support formatted text.

**Progression / coaching.** None in the classic sense: no auto-program generator or set-by-set recommendations confirmed. Coaching value comes from historical numbers, records, RPE and warm-up/plate tools.

**Progress & stats.** Exercise detail with all-time bests, best at each rep and projected best at rep ranges. Records screen, 1RM and volume charts, body measurements, muscle heat map in marketing, CSV export and Apple Health sync.

**Gamification.** PR notifications and share sheets for workouts/templates; custom icons and themes in Pro. No ranks, leaderboards or social feed.

**Home / navigation / design.** Workout/History/Progress style navigation with a deliberate tools-down orientation; restrained, neutral visual design. Exact palette and typography `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free: unlimited workouts, 3 custom routines. Pro $4.99/mo or $29.99/yr: unlimited routines/templates, body measurements, all charts, plate and warm-up calculators, custom icons/themes.

## 4. Fitbod

**Positioning.** AI-personalized strength training that plans every session. Users praise no decision fatigue and consistency; criticism is that progression can feel repetitive, random or conservative.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** `Workout` tab → tap an exercise → Exercise Details shows name, video/instructions (GIFs removed; offline video unavailable), recommended sets/reps/weight, history, per-arm/leg labels, notes, rest timer and RiR. Tap reps or weight to type values; `+Add Set`; swipe a set left to delete. `More > Add Warm-Up Set`; `More > Build Superset/Circuit`.

**Set types & tools.** RiR is prompted after each set, or after all sets of a superset/circuit. RiR scale: 4+ very easy, 3 moderate, 2 hypertrophy target, 1 near failure, 0 failure. Rest timer icon sits below the exercise name near the set count; duration is generated from exercise difficulty and lasts only for that workout, separate from device notifications. Plate calculator: barbell button next to History, or swipe right on any set; supports many plate-loaded machines and shows the nearest loadable weight. Warm-up sets via More menu. Changing weight/reps feeds the algorithm. Saved workouts can keep sets/reps/weight, regenerate sets/reps/weight, or regenerate weight only.

**Progression / coaching.** `My Plan` takes goal, experience, a hard equipment filter, split, duration and preferences. The Exercise Selector scores 800+ (later 1600+) exercises using effectiveness data from 87M+/400M+ logged workouts, equipment, muscle recovery on a 0–100% scale, variety, and explicit feedback (“Recommend More Often”, “Less”, “Don't Recommend Again”). mStrength adjusts intensity and volume. Estimated Strength and Max Effort Days use ramp-ups plus two AMRAP sets marked “+ reps” to recalibrate strength.

**Progress & stats.** `Body` tab: muscle recovery heat map, PRs, volume, tonnage, performance trends and Estimated Strength. `Log` tab: completed history. Exact PR taxonomy `unverified`.

**Gamification.** Weak: streak/consistency framing and PRs, no ranks, leaderboards or quests confirmed.

**Home / navigation / design.** Four tabs: Workout, Log, Body, My Plan. Exercise-detail-heavy flow with video/instruction panel and recommendation prompts. Exact palette `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Subscription-only; 7-day trial requires a subscription. Website pricing $15.99/mo or $95.99/year. Offline mode exists, but offline video does not.

## 5. Alpha Progression

**Positioning.** “Build muscle, build strength.” Free logger and manual plans; Pro generates precise plans and set-by-set recommendations. Review themes: flexibility, precise progression, real form videos.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** Current-exercise-focused logger with a horizontal thumbnail tab header of completed exercises. Set rows show target and actual weight/reps, RIR and a performance metric — estimated 1RM, estimated 10RM or volume — with previous sets and notes visible. The “Picker” sorts likely values before keyboard entry. Exact column order `unverified`.

**Set types & tools.** Rest timer auto-starts with an alarm; duration by exercise type with per-exercise overrides, countdown continues past zero, iOS Live Activity/Android notification. Supersets and dropsets; drop weight is calculated from the final normal working set. Temporary and pinned notes. Library of 795 exercise videos. Pro warm-up calculator customizable by compound/medium/isolation and cold/warm muscles, with percentages such as 35%/65% and copy-to-notes. Pro plate calculator in the weight picker suggests the next lower achievable weight if the exact load is impossible. RIR supports half-steps (e.g. 1.5). Works almost entirely offline; generators need internet; no watch app.

**Progression / coaching.** Set-by-set weight/reps/(optional RIR) recommendations use previous workouts, sets completed today and the planned set. The app knows available weights and suggests more reps before an impractical dumbbell jump (7.5 → 10 kg), avoiding a reload for negligible improvement. No recommendation on first performance; recommendations can move downward after worse performance; deloads adjust. RIR is optional; all suggestions are editable.

**Plan generator (Pro).** Inputs: gender, experience, gym, goal (max strength, muscle gain, strength endurance), muscle priorities, frequency/duration, split — auto or full body / upper-lower / push-pull / PPL / PPL-upper-lower. Generates 4–7 week plans or indefinite plans with optional periodization and deload. Multiple gym profiles (Pro); equipment ranges with bar + plates; exercise whitelist/blacklist.

**Progress & stats.** PRs for heaviest weight, most reps, highest volume, highest estimated 1RM/10RM and best muscle-group performance. Pro charts: exercise performance, strength, volume, sets per muscle, workouts per week, bodyweight and body measurements. Weekly target streak (1–7 workouts/week) plus consecutive-day streak; achievements with punny badges and trophies every 10%; weekly/monthly/yearly reports.

**Gamification.** Streaks, achievements, badges, trophies and reports — quieter than Liftoff.

**Home / navigation / design.** Workout-centric with horizontal exercise strip and dense set rows; information-rich rather than playful. Exact design tokens `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free, no ads, no account required: unlimited logging, manual plans, 795 videos, rest timer, records, streaks, achievements, body measurements, sharing, CSV export. Pro: generator, recommendations, RIR, periodization, deloads, plate/warm-up calculators, multiple gyms, exercise evaluations, custom images, charts; 14-day trial.

## 6. Boostcamp

**Positioning.** Free workout tracker plus 14,000+ programs; App Store 4.9/11K ratings. Review themes: structured programs drive consistency; BarBend tester used it for 2+ years.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** Tapping a set logs it and auto-starts the timer. Last logged weight is shown; units switch lb/kg per exercise. RPE uses 6–10 in half-points with RIR hints (“Could do 2 more reps”). A live PR flag appears in-session. Plate calculator: 10 free uses, unlimited in Pro. Warm-up templates; supersets and drop sets. Swap Exercise preserves sets/reps/RPE/%1RM. Rest timer defaults or per exercise, in 5 s steps up to 60 min or off, with presets 30 s / 1 / 2 / 3 / 5 min / custom; Live Activity and Dynamic Island on iOS, notification on Android. Rep ranges, RPE ranges and timed exercises supported. Exact set row columns and keypad layout `unverified`.

**Progression / coaching.** Auto Progression (Pro) raises weight when reps are hit and adjusts after missed reps. Personalized Programs (Pro) use a questionnaire. Free library: 130+ coach-designed and 14,000+ community programs.

**Progress & stats.** Lifetime and This Week PR boards. Free Strength Score 0–100 based on DOTS across squat, deadlift, horizontal press, vertical press and pull. Pro adds Strength Score Level/Max/Balance, Muscle Tracker (front/back heatmap, radar, table) and Exercise Analytics/Records/Charts (best set, estimated and actual 1RM, max set volume, best 1–12 reps). Free Weekly Report covers workouts, time, tonnage, PRs, muscles and streak. Progress photos and bodyweight averaged charts compare up to four series.

**Gamification.** PR boards, Strength Score, weekly reports, community feed with reactions/comments and share cards. No ranks/XP confirmed.

**Home / navigation / design.** Grouped as Programs, Train, Analytics, Community. Flat card-based modern design; exact colors and typography `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free: programs (most coach programs), full tracker, RPE, rest timers, PR boards, supersets/dropsets/warmups, Swap Exercise, Weekly Report, Strength Score view, 10 plate-calculator uses, unlimited manual custom programs, 3 custom exercises. Pro from $4.99/mo or $59.99/yr, 7-day trial: 20+ premium programs, Strength Score Level/Max/Balance, Muscle Tracker, Personalized Programs, Auto Progression, Exercise Analytics/Charts, unlimited plate calculator and custom exercises. Offline once a program is loaded.

## 7. JEFIT

**Positioning.** All-in-one gym workout tracker claiming “deepest training analysis”; 13M lifters, 1,400+ exercises; App Store 4.8 with 47K ratings. Review themes: big library, plans and logging; UI described as cluttered/generic. A vendor comparison claims JEFIT no longer surfaces a straightforward manual logger; that claim is third-party and `unverified`.

**Active workout / entry mechanics.** Workout tab → pick a day → Start Workout. Each set is pre-filled with last time's values; estimated 1RM shows on every set and immediate PR notices fire. Exercise summary shows duration, volume, records and muscles trained; top-right exercise menu opens HD demo (source inconsistency on HD vs standard video, `unverified`).

**Set types & tools.** Warm-up, working, failure and drop sets; supersets; self-starting rest timer; exercise notes. Exact set row layout and keypad `unverified` — official pages were blocked by a cookie wall, so these details come from search snippets.

**Progression / coaching.** Adaptive Plan is a 12-week plan adjusting weekly from logged sets, goal, level and equipment, with build/peak/deload mesocycles. Load Progression (Elite) suggests next weight/reps; Periodization is Elite. FAQ claims the algorithm is built on 13M+ user data.

**Progress & stats.** 1RM on every set, 1RM chart, PRs, full exercise history, volume and workout time, streaks, workout calendar, muscle breakdown and recovery chart, body stats and progress photos. Elite adds Progress Index, JEFIT Insights, 1RM analysis, interactive BodyMap, muscle balance and weekly sets per muscle. Community, challenges and PR sharing.

**Gamification.** Streaks, PRs, challenges, community feed and milestone sharing; no XP/rank system confirmed.

**Home / navigation / design.** Workout/plan-centric with many entry points and dense secondary navigation; visually the most cluttered of the seven. Exact palette `unverified`.

**Paywall split.** Free Basic: logging, supersets, rest timers, 1,400+ exercises with demos/instructions, full history, cloud backup, community, muscle recovery, 1RM/PRs, 2 plans, 7 custom exercises, 3 watch sessions. Elite: unlimited plans/custom exercises/watch, Adaptive Plan, Load Progression, Periodization, advanced analytics, 400+ coach plans, ad-free.

## Top 20 ideas to adopt (free, offline, single-user)

Ranked by value for a free offline single-user Flutter logbook. Ideas that need servers or social network effects are excluded.

1. **Inline previous set** in every row — the single biggest friction reducer across Hevy, Strong, Boostcamp and JEFIT.
2. **One-tap set completion that starts the rest timer** — Strong and Boostcamp make this the default path.
3. **Set-type tags on the set number** (warm-up / normal / failure / drop); warm-ups excluded from volume and records, as in Strong.
4. **Per-exercise rest duration with per-set override**, countdown continuing past zero (Alpha), plus sensible presets.
5. **RPE/RIR as optional input, not a gate** — Strong's 6–10 and Alpha's half-step RIR show both styles; never block logging.
6. **Plate calculator in the weight field**, honoring bar weight and available plates; nearest loadable fallback from Alpha.
7. **Last-time-based progression suggestions** that prefer extra reps before an impractical weight jump (Alpha's 7.5 → 10 kg insight).
8. **Editable recommendations, downward moves allowed** — recommendations must never be authoritative or mandatory.
9. **Manual routines with unlimited sets/exercises and zero account requirement** — the free tier should be genuinely complete (Alpha, Strong).
10. **PR detection live in the session**, with record types: heaviest weight, most reps, best volume, best estimated 1RM (Hevy, Alpha, Boostcamp).
11. **Exercise detail with best-set / best-at-rep / projected 1RM** — cheap to compute offline, high perceived value.
12. **Simple weekly/monthly calendar with streak**, configurable target days 1–7; no social grace mechanics needed (Alpha).
13. **Sets-per-muscle tally and lightweight body diagram** — derived from logged sets, no server.
14. **Pinned + temporary notes** with links, per exercise and per session (Hevy, Alpha).
15. **Warm-up set generation** from a configurable formula, copyable into notes; do not paywall it.
16. **Supersets with a clear visual connector and “next set” navigation** (Strong, Hevy).
17. **CSV export and local full-history access from day one** — Strong and Alpha treat data ownership as a feature.
18. **Bodyweight and measurement tracking with simple trend charts** — free in Alpha and Strong.
19. **Weekly report from local data**: sessions, time, tonnage, PRs, muscles, streak (Boostcamp).
20. **Achievements/badges with local milestones** — optional, cheap, and should never require an account or purchases.

Explicitly avoid: ranks tied to bodyweight/sex, leaderboards, XP shops, paid streak restores, AI plan subscriptions, and cloud-only history.
